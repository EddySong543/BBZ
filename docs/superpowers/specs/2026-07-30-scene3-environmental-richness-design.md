# Scene3 Environmental Richness Design

## Goal

Complete approved richness tasks 2, 3, and 4 without adding external assets or
changing the authored Scene3 composition.

## Protected State

- Keep the current sun, cloud sea, mountains, cliffs, chains, sword-grave
  cluster transforms, and `battle_screen3.tscn` character geometry unchanged.
- Keep all effects Scene3-only.
- Preserve nearest-neighbor pixel presentation.
- Keep motion low-frequency and staggered so the battle platform remains the
  primary readable plane.

## Task 2: Cloud-Driven Cliff Light

Extend the active Scene3 cliff grass shader instead of adding duplicate cliff
sprites. A broad screen-space light band and a softer trailing shadow band move
over opaque cliff pixels. Their timing reuses the `0.42` roll and `0.82` billow
rhythms already used by the Scene3 cloud and ray materials. Left and right
cliffs use different phase offsets, so they do not brighten in unison.

## Task 3: Two-Depth Sun Motes

Use two sparse `GPUParticles2D` layers with generated `GradientTexture2D`
subresources:

- Far layer: nine small, slow motes immediately above the sun-ray field.
- Near layer: six slightly larger, faster motes above the sword-grave layer and
  below the cloud sea.

Both emit only through the central ray region, use low-alpha warm colors, and
retain local coordinates so BattleStage parallax does not detach live
particles from their layer.

## Task 4: Staggered Sword Glints

Attach one procedural additive glint to a selected sword in each sword-grave
cluster. Each glint is a small pixel-stepped cross generated by a Scene3-only
shader. The two cycles are longer than eight seconds and use different phases,
so only one can appear at a time during normal observation.

## Verification

- A focused GUT test loads only `scene3.tscn`, avoiding unrelated battle-system
  compilation work.
- Godot import must report no scene or shader parse errors.
- Three timed Scene3 screenshots must show changing cliff illumination and
  mote positions without disturbing composition.
- The full BattleScreen3 probe must preserve platform/world parallax sync.
