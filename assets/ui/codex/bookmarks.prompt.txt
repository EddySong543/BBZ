Built-in ImageGen mode, generated as two separate transparent source images.

Chapter source: one blank horizontal parchment page-edge index tab, front-on and approximately 2.1:1. Straight top and bottom, a clean stepped exposed end, warm beige paper, dark coffee outer pixel border, restrained tan inner line. No text, icon, shadow, ornament, pattern, blur, gradient, noise, or extra objects.

Rarity source: one blank compact secondary parchment index tab, front-on and approximately 2.6:1. Simpler and thinner than the chapter tab, with the same restrained paper and coffee border language. No text, rarity stripe, icon, shadow, ornament, pattern, blur, gradient, noise, or extra objects.

Both sources were requested on a flat #FF00FF chroma-key background, but ImageGen returned native transparency. `tools/prepare_codex_bookmarks.py` trims, quantizes, hardens alpha, reduces to a logical pixel grid, and upscales exactly 2x with nearest-neighbour sampling.
